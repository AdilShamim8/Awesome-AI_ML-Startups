### Key Trends

AI is transforming FinTech across multiple dimensions: (1) AI-powered payments infrastructure with companies like Stripe integrating AI for fraud detection and automated operations; (2) AI-driven financial advisory making wealth management accessible to retail investors; (3) a new category of FinTech infrastructure built specifically for AI agents, exemplified by Maven's payments layer for voice agents; and (4) climate-focused FinTech that routes capital toward environmental outcomes. The convergence of AI agents and financial transactions is creating entirely new market segments.
