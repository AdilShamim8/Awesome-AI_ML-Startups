### Key Trends

AI-native fintech decoupled from the 2021-22 valuation reset, and October set a new high-water mark: Ramp's $1.85B primary at $60B (36% above June) with $2B+ ARR makes it the most valuable private fintech outside Stripe. Consolidation arrived from both directions — Capital One bought Brex, Stripe finalized its $7.5B OpenRouter acquisition to put model routing inside payments — while agent-payments infrastructure (Maven's PCI-compliant APIs for voice agents) emerged as the newest sub-sector. IPO pipelines (Stripe, Plaid) should reshape the public fintech landscape in 2027.
