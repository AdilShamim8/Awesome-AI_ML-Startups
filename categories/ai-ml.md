### Key Trends

The AI infrastructure stack absorbed the majority of 2026's non-lab funding, and the frontier widened geographically and technologically. Inference economics now drive the biggest checks: inference clouds (Fireworks at $17.5B, Baseten at $13B, Together AI at $8.3B), GPU/energy clouds (Crusoe's $3.9B Series F at $30.9B), and purpose-built silicon (Etched's Sohu at $21B, with $40-50B offers reported). New archetypes appeared in October: TypeSafe AI's decision-output models (an $870M Series A at $7.5B weeks after launch) and sovereign research labs in Japan (Sakana) and India (Sarvam). Scale AI's post-Meta trajectory — $1B revenue expected in 2026 and a Pentagon enterprise agreement expanded to $500M — shows data infrastructure maturing into defense-grade business.
