### Key Trends

The generative AI landscape in 2026 is defined by three forces: (1) the continued dominance of foundation model labs like OpenAI, Anthropic, and xAI that are raising unprecedented rounds; (2) the rise of specialized generative AI for verticals like code (Poolside) and creative content (Runway); and (3) the emergence of European and global players challenging US hegemony, led by Mistral AI. Consumer AI companions like Romantic AI represent a growing market segment.
