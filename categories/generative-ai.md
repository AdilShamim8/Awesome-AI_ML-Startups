### Key Trends

Foundation-model financing reached unprecedented scale in 2026 — and then globalized. OpenAI ($122B, Mar), Anthropic ($65B, May), and xAI ($20B, Jan — before merging into SpaceX) concentrated capital at the top, while China's open-weight labs arrived as a new pole: DeepSeek ($7.4B first external round at $50B+), Moonshot AI (~$2B at $20B), and the Hong Kong listings of Z.ai and MiniMax created a public-market window for Chinese frontier AI. Sovereign AI became a business model (Mistral's €3B Series D, Sarvam's India stack), and specialists converted vertical excellence into multi-billion marks (ElevenLabs' $22B tender, Suno at $5.4B). The October watch item: Anthropic's IPO — the first frontier-lab listing — with marketing expected the week of November 9, 2026.
