### Key Trends

AI in healthcare is moving from diagnostic assistance to full workflow automation. The YC W26 batch includes multiple HealthTech companies building AI agents that don't just assist healthcare workers but autonomously handle routine tasks like patient intake, scheduling, and insurance verification. This shift from "AI as a tool" to "AI as an autonomous worker" in healthcare is driven by severe staffing shortages and the need to reduce administrative burden on clinicians. The HealthTech sector saw $29.7B in funding in the HealthTech 250 cluster, with AI-powered diagnostics and documentation leading investment.
