### Key Trends

Vertical SaaS is being rebuilt around AI-native workflows, and the October window showed the money following proof: EliseAI's $350M and Pigment's $250M at $2.3B both reward companies with established recurring revenue rather than promises. The fastest growers sell outcomes — revenue, sourced spend, filled units — instead of seats, and agentic buyers are emerging as a customer class that purchases via APIs. Evaluation and observability (LMArena, Momentic) remains must-have infrastructure as enterprises move AI into production.
